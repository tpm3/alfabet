Frequently Asked Questions & Common Issues

  • Can I change my name or callsign in the app? Yes - use the Settings app.
  • Can I turn the firework display off? Yes - use the Settings app.
  • How do I report a bug? Please email us with a complete description of the issue, what screen you were on, and the specific device you were using.
  • How can I request a feature? We love feedback! Send your ideas straight to our email.
  • Where is my data stored? Whiskey Tango operates entirely locally on your device. There are no accounts created, and we do not collect, or store any of your personal data on external servers.
