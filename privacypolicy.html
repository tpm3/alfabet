This policy applies to the handling of information about the user that is used by the app. The policy refers to such a user as “you” in this policy document.

What the app collects and why

The primary purpose of the app is to help amateur radio users learn or improve their fluency with using the NATO alphabet. The guiding principle is to collect only what is needed. Here’s what that means in practice:

Identity

When you run the app for the first time, you are asked to provide a first name, and an optional amateur radio call sign. The name is used to provide a friendly greeting. If the call sign is provided, it is included in the internal vocabulary of words used in the core educational function of the app.

Both pieces of information are persisted on your device. If at a later date you wish to modify or completely remove this information, you can do so directly via the Settings app. Neither piece of information is checked or verified against any external databases.

This personal information never leaves your device and is never sold to third parties.

Geolocation Data

Every time the app starts it will attempt to determine your geographic location via standard iOS location services. If you allow it and it is successful, the app will add both the four and six character Maidenhead Square geocode to the internal vocabulary of words used by the core educational function of the app.

This geocode information is not persisted. It never leaves your device and is never sold to third parties.

Sensor Data

One of the four activities of the app uses the microphone to capture the users voice then immediately perform speech recognition on that data as part of the core educational function of the app.

This voice data exists transiently in memory and is never persisted, it never leaves your device, and is never sold to third parties.

Performance Logs

The application creates and maintains an on-device log of your accuracy and pace on the various activities it provides. The information is retained for a period of up to 90 days, and can be deleted on a per-activity basis at any time.

Voluntary Correspondence

If you email iosalfabetapp@gmail.com with a question or to ask for help, we keep that correspondence, including your email address, so that we have a past history of correspondence to reference if you reach out in future.

How we secure your data

The app only persists the first name and optional call sign, these are protected by standard iOS mechanisms. All other data is held transiently in memory and will be deleted when the app terminates.

Third Parties

No third party code is used. No data is transmitted off the device by the app.

Changes and questions

If we make significant changes to the app or the policy, we will refresh the date at the top of this page.
