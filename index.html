About the App

Whiskey Tango is a clean, minimal application for learning and improving fluency with the NATO alphabet, designed for iOS. It is engineered with user privacy at its core, operating without tracking or external servers.

Features

  • Four activities: Speak, sort, type and listen to NATO alphabet words.
  • Track your progress: Accumulated scores plus pace and accuracy graphs.
  • 100% Local: All data is stored directly on your device.
  • No Accounts: Start using the application instantly without a login.
